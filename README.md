## 👋 About Me

I'm an **Actuarial Manager and Chartered Enterprise Risk Analyst (CERA)** with 9+ years of actuarial experience and 5+ years of management and leadership experience in the financial services and insurance industry.

My work sits at the intersection of actuarial science, financial risk management and technology. I'm particularly interested in using software engineering, automation and artificial intelligence to solve complex financial and actuarial problems.

### 🎯 Professional Interests

- **Actuarial Valuations:** Life insurance valuations, IFRS 17, embedded value and financial reporting.
- **Capital Management:** Solvency Assessment and Management (SAM), Solvency II, capital optimisation and regulatory capital.
- **Risk Management:** Enterprise risk management, financial risk, quantitative modelling and capital adequacy.
- **Leadership:** Building high-performing teams, developing talent and delivering complex actuarial programmes.
- **Technology Transformation:** Actuarial automation, data engineering, modernisation and digital transformation.
- **Artificial Intelligence:** Local AI, machine learning, intelligent automation and quantitative applications.
